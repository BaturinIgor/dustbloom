# Sound credits

All sound effects come from [Kenney](https://kenney.nl) packs released under
CC0 1.0 (public domain): Interface Sounds, RPG Audio, Casino Audio, Music
Jingles. The original files and licences are in `source/kenney/`.
`build_sfx.py` softens, trims and levels them into `client/Dustbloom/Sounds`.

`generate_sfx.py` is the old synthesized set (stage 0); it is no longer used.
